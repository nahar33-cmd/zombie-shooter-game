# 🧟 Zombie Shooter Game

Play as a zombie with sharp claws fighting against waves of armed human enemies!

## 🎮 Game Overview

You are a green zombie with glowing yellow eyes and razor-sharp red claws. Your mission is to survive waves of increasingly difficult human enemies who are armed with guns. Use your agility and melee combat skills to take them down before they shoot you!

## 🎯 Objective

- **Survive** waves of human enemies
- **Attack** enemies with your zombie claws
- **Dodge** incoming gunfire
- **Reach** high scores by defeating enemies efficiently
- **Progress** through increasingly difficult waves

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action | Keys |
|--------|------|
| Move | `WASD` or `Arrow Keys` |
| Aim | Move your `Mouse` |
| Attack (Claws) | `Left Click` |

## 🎮 Gameplay Mechanics

### Player (Zombie)
- **Health**: 100 HP
- **Attack Range**: 50 pixels (melee)
- **Attack Damage**: 20 damage per hit
- **Attack Speed**: Quick cooldown between swipes
- **Special Ability**: Sharp claws with area-of-effect damage

### Enemies (Humans)
- **Appearance**: Orange-colored humans with guns
- **Behavior**: Chase the player and shoot continuously
- **Health**: Increases with each wave (starts at 30 HP)
- **Damage**: 10 damage per bullet
- **Wave Progression**: More enemies spawn as waves increase

### Game Progression
- **Wave System**: Defeat all enemies to advance to the next wave
- **Difficulty Scaling**: Enemy health increases by 10 for each wave
- **Enemy Count**: Increases by 1 for each wave
- **Scoring**: 100 points × current level for each enemy defeated

## 🎨 Visual Features

- **Grid-based arena**: 800×600 pixel canvas
- **Particle effects**: Blood particles on hits, attack effects
- **Health bars**: Visual display of player and enemy health
- **Real-time HUD**: Score, kills, wave count, and level display
- **Aim indicator**: Green line showing your current aim direction
- **Attack range preview**: Red circle showing your attack area when attacking

## 📊 UI Elements

- **Zombie Health Bar**: Your current health status (top-left)
- **Wave Indicator**: Current wave and enemy count (center)
- **Score Display**: Total kills and current level (right side)
- **Enemy Health**: Nearest enemy's health status

## 🚀 How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in a modern web browser
2. The game starts immediately in Wave 1
3. Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move around the arena
4. Click to attack enemies with your claws (short range)
5. Dodge incoming bullets from the human enemies
6. Defeat all enemies in a wave to advance
7. Survive as many waves as possible
8. Click "RESTART GAME" when defeated to play again

## 💡 Tips & Strategies

- **Keep Moving**: Don't stay in one place - enemies will focus fire on you
- **Close the Distance**: Get close to enemies before attacking
- **Use Claws Efficiently**: Your claws have a cooldown, time your attacks
- **Watch Your Health**: Health bars above enemies show their remaining health
- **Observe Patterns**: Enemies move toward you predictably - use this to your advantage
- **Area Control**: Use the arena boundaries strategically
